# Narrative → beats

1. **Strip the ask to one sentence.** "We're launching a lighter version of the product" → "The same thing, half the weight."
2. **Find the literal picture.** What does that sentence LOOK like taken literally? An object on a scale that
   tips. The best ideas are physical (a fold, a drop, a stretch, a seam closing). Reject ideas that need a
   caption to explain them.
3. **Check it's TRUE before you fall in love.** Every claim the picture makes needs a source. No numbers you can't cite.
4. **Acts (3-4).** A common arc: *studio* (the idea as an event on a clean ground) → *proof* (the range, the
   detail) → *world* (real life, real photos) → *card* (the line, the sign-off, a held end frame).
5. **Beat table, in BRIEF.md, before building:**

| beat | t | visual | words (approved copy) | sound |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0.5 | 0.25 | label settles | Season one | tick |
| 1-3 | 0.5-1.5 | headline, one line per beat | … | type ×3 |
| 8 (drop) | 4.0 | wipe → the proof | … | wipe |
| 16 | 8.0 | wipe → world, word behind the subject | WORD. | boom |
| 22 | 11.0 | wipe → end card, hold ≥ 1 s | Brand · line | line, chime |

6. **Assets → prep.sh.** One command per asset. If an asset can't be made honestly, change the beat, not the rule.
7. **Before publishing:** Is every word approved? Does every giant word READ in the full-resolution frame? Do fast
   moves streak smoothly instead of double-printing? Is anything under the app's UI? Would someone clock a
   one-frame pop, a jump, or a fallback font?
